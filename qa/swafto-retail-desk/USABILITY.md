# Usability and accessibility pass — SWAFTO Retail Desk (QA wave 4, on device)

Tester: usability-accessibility-tester · 2026-11-18 · vivo I2219, Android 16, 1080x2408 · build: debug APK from `productionize`.
Evidence: `qa/swafto-retail-desk/evidence/U-*.png` (70 files). The R1-* files from the device tester were used only for the sign-in screens (A1 to A4, X0, X1), which I did not re-drive because that needs a logout.

## How this was done, and what it is not
- High-contrast text was ON throughout (owner's setting, left on). It draws a white outline round dark text and a dark outline round light text, and forces text cores to pure black or white in many places. I did not count the outlines against the app. Ratios below are therefore for the rendered pixels with the setting on; the app's own ink colours without the setting could not be measured here.
- Pixel ratios: sampled from the screenshots (background = most common colour in the box, text = most common clearly different colour), computed with the WCAG relative-luminance formula.
- Targets: from uiautomator bounds, converted at density 440. Judged against the registry floor (the CTO's correction: the project floor, not the generic one). Measured values are quoted as measured.
- Not done: TalkBack (it would change the owner's accessibility settings), keyboard focus order, a real user. Everything about "what a shopkeeper would do" is my judgement; the 5-user test is still owed. Times below are my estimates, not measurements.
- State I changed on the test book (synthetic): one Rs 50 cash sale and one Rs 1 cash sale (the second recorded offline, then synced); on the 19 Nov book I tapped "Nothing to enter" on sections 2 to 8, so Today now reads "9 of 10 / READY". I did NOT close the day. Font scale, language (English), `adb reverse` and the clock are restored to what I found. No `pm clear`, install or file deletion. `pm path com.swafto` was non-empty throughout.

## Known/pending seen on screen (not re-filed)
- Phone-date notice (QA-06): the notice is a full white card, four lines, above everything, and pushes the close card and tiles down by about a fifth of the screen. It reads clearly ("Nothing is lost. Check the phone's date and time.") but offers no button to open the phone's date settings. At 2.0x it is eight lines.
- Over-collection (QA-03) shows as "Udhar to collect -Rs 456.00" on Today, Ledger, Reports and the Udhar book, while Reports also says "No udhar is open". The same screen contradicts itself, with no explanation of a negative.
- Mode not named on list rows (QA-05), refusal message leaking into the next form (QA-07): both reproduced (see U-06, U-11, U-15).

## 4. Prioritised redesign list (top 10)

Each item says what changes, why, and for whom (the shopkeeper: middle-aged to elderly, Odia first, one hand, shop daylight, cheap phone).

1. **Today: sale first.** Put four large tiles (Sale, Udhar sale, Collect, Expense) directly under the header, above every figure; move Purchase and Pay into a "More" tile. Make "Continue day close" a secondary tonal button until the shop's own closing time, then promote it. Why: the job done forty times a day is recording a sale; he currently scrolls past a screen of figures first. For: every user, one hand.
2. **Keep Today's scroll position (or never need one).** Return to the same place after a form; better, make the tiles sticky above the figures. Why: five or six actions per sale becomes three. For: shop counter, customer waiting.
3. **One hero on Today: "In the box now".** One big number with its working in one line ("Opening + cash today"), then a single sentence for sales and udhar. Hide zero tiles ("Nothing paid out yet"). Why: today six same-size tiles hide the one figure he wants and the zeros take half of them. For: a numerate, non-accounting reader.
4. **Fix the Udhar book as three screens with the right titles, no stale error, and balances.** Titles "Udhar sale", "Collect", "Pay supplier"; never show a refusal before a tap and clear it on leaving; in the picker show each person's balance owed in large type, then show the chosen person's balance above the keypad; show a calm "this is more than they owe" confirm for an over-collection (QA-03) instead of a silent negative. Why: this is the money screen the earlier audit and QA-03/QA-07 point at. For: collecting udhar.
5. **Rebuild Ledger as "Parties" with balances first.** Name the tab and the title the same word; list people sorted by amount owed, name large and balance larger, "changed today" as a small line; "+ Customer" in the header; tapping a person opens a page whose hero is the balance, with one primary button ("Collect") and a quiet "New udhar sale". Why: "how much does he owe" is the main question. For: udhar collection.
6. **Make Add the strongest control on every entry form.** Full-width marigold bar directly above the keypad on the thumb side, always legible as a button even at zero (disabled look reserved for a real disabled state with a one-line reason on tap); after saving, show the new row at the top of the list with its mode and time, plus a short "Saved Rs 50 cash". Why: the Add button is currently the smallest and most faded control and the save gives almost no feedback. For: one-handed entry.
7. **Day close: whole row opens the form; "Nothing today" becomes a quiet link with a one-tap undo; show a "skipped" count.** Replace the loud pill; show which count "N of 10" includes; show the cash-check and Close rows as visible locked lines with a lock cue; keep the sticky next-step button (it is the best part). Why: the loudest control on every row is the one that lets him skip. For: the 9 pm close.
8. **Make sync state a persistent chip.** Header chip: green tick "Saved", amber clock "3 waiting - safe on phone" (never red), tap explains in his language; show it on every screen, not as grey text mid-page. Why: for a user who has lost a paper book, "safe on phone but not on the server" is the state he needs to see. For: weak or no network.
9. **Let the text-size choice be real.** Cap chrome only; allow body text to 2.0x with one-column reflow; keep the working list on Cash check scrollable at full height; add "Normal / Large / Extra large" with a live preview in onboarding. Fix collisions: "TODAY SO FAR / All saved on phone", "Should be" and amount, the Day close ring at 2.0x. Why: those who pick the largest size get a broken layout. For: elderly users and daylight.
10. **Odia/Hindi layout pass with the native reviewer (H13 stays open).** Stop fixed-height pills clipping words (Hindi skip label); allow two lines in skip pills and tiles; show the Odia date's numerals in a clear typeface or confirm 19 is read as 19; replace "Cash check / box / Net sales" with the words he uses once the reviewer confirms them. Why: a cut-off "नहीं" reverses the meaning of the skip button. For: Hindi and Odia users.

Also queued, lower priority: replace the `Help and contact` developer sentence with a real contact when the human item closes; show the https address (not localhost) on About; rename the delete button; make switches look like switches and give withdrawal a one-tap path; align hardware Back with in-app Back and ask before leaving from Today; add an icon set so non-readers can scan Settings and the six tiles.

## 5. Honest answer
Tidy is not good here. The design system is careful (big keypad, calm copy, clear day-close next step, clean Odia and Hindi rendering, no colour-only meaning, ratios and target sizes met on every sample). It is organised around closing the day and the audit trail, not around the forty sales and the collection he does, and the udhar screens give him wrong titles, a stale error and no balances. Those, and the Hindi skip label that reads as its opposite, are what he would hit first.
