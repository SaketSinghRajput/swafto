Ratios are computed with the WCAG relative-luminance formula by the generator script that wrote this file; any pair under its threshold stops generation. Large-text threshold 3:1 is not relied on anywhere: every text pair is held to 4.5:1.

Ground: every concept uses a warm, non-white ground. #F7F1E3 (A), #FFFBEA (B), #FFF6EA (C); pure white is only a card. Reason (assumption, to be confirmed by the M4 daylight test on the real phone): an all-white screen is the brightest possible surface in a sunlit shop, and the audit records that the current warm paper was chosen to cut that glare. A is the darkest ground (relative luminance 0.882, against 0.952 for today's #FFF9EF) and is the largest daylight change proposed; B and C stay within 0.962 to 0.931.

Type

Anek Odia only. An earlier draft used Baloo Bhaina 2 for friendliness; it is dropped because the audit makes a second Odia family a check:font change and about +490 KB, and money must stay in the Odia face. Friendliness comes from shape, tint and pictograms instead. Bold for money and labels, Medium for body, leading 1.8, tabular figures, no tracking, no all-caps.

Icons

Icon font (audit approach A): about 20 glyphs (coin, notebook, tray, receipt, dots, box, bars, note, phone, user, tick, dash, lock, clock, check, chevron, back, backspace, plus, up/down). Cost: one new static .ttf in assets/fonts (size not measured; ASSUMPTION 30-60 KB), a check:font and fonts-packaged generalisation, a licence check, and a test double. Every pictogram sits beside its word and takes its accessible name from the existing string. Colour is a third cue, never the only one.

Shape, edge and depth

Soft: radius 20 on cards and tiles, 24 on the header, hero and tab bar, pill (28-32) on buttons, segments and chips; 48 dp pictogram discs; circular avatar initials in the Ledger. Depth by tint only (5 tint pairs per mode), no elevation, no shadow, no gradient. 2 px edges at 3:1 on controls.

Why this for this user

This direction is for the owner who reads slowly, or whose helper or daughter reads for him. A coin, a notebook, a hand-and-tray and a receipt let him find Sale, Udhar sale, Collect and Expense by shape before he reads the word, and each also has its own tint so the four jobs stay apart even at arm's length. Soft rounded shapes and warm cream make the app feel like a helper rather than an accounts system, which matters for adoption by someone who is slightly afraid of getting money wrong. The Sale tile is the only solid teal block, so the one-hand, forty-times-a-day action is still first. Trust is kept by the figures, which are plain, large and signed; the friendliness is in the frame, not in the numbers.

Risks

  • Icon font is the one real cost: a new asset, a check change (prove:verifiers), a licence review, and 20 glyphs to keep consistent. If the CTO declines, only the View-drawable marks survive and this direction loses its point.
  • Five tint pairs times two modes are new state colours that must be added to tokens.spec with the same names in both modes.
  • Five hues can become a rainbow; decorative colour must not be read as meaning (no tile hue means "good" or "bad").
  • Odia initials in avatar discs need a check on conjunct-initial names (a first code point is not always a whole letter).
  • Large radii and many rounded Views cost slightly more to draw than rectangles on a low-end GPU, but there are no shadows or blur.

Audit yardstick

With changes: needs an icon font (about 20 glyphs, bytes unmeasured, ASSUMPTION 30-60 KB; check:font and fonts-packaged generalised; licence review; test double); five new tint pairs per mode in tokens; radius 20/28 tokens (xl 24 exists); hero 36 and a figure-leading token as in A. Anek only, so no second Odia family.

Mockup honesty: rendered in a browser with Google Fonts Anek Odia (variable), not on the Android phone. Layout, Odia rendering at 1.5x/2.0x and tabular figures on device are M2/M3 checks.
