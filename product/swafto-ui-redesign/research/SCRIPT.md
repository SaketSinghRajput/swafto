# SCRIPT: moderator script for one session

Status: DRAFT for Saket. English is the master. **Key prompts are also given in Odia and Hindi: both are DRAFT and need a native reviewer** (repo rule H13). Say them in your own natural words if you speak the language better than the draft does; the meaning matters, not the wording. Things in [BRACKETS] are choices to fill.

Total time: about 70 minutes (consent 10, warm-up 8, think-aloud practice 3, tasks 35, SUS 5, debrief 7, close 2). If the participant is tired or the shop is busy, drop T1b and the optional probes first, then T7. Never cut consent.

## 0. Before they arrive (10 minutes)

- Checklist in `SETUP.md` complete: test phone in state R1, brightness at max, stopwatch ready, printed task cards in order, consent copies in the participant's language, observation sheet printed with their code, thank-you ready.
- Put the phone face down until you start.
- **You are not the app's inventor today.** If you are Saket or a company person, say: "I work with the team that made this, but please be honest. Whatever is hard in the app is a problem we must fix, and I will not be upset." People say "it is good" to the person who made it. Ask for the hard parts.
- Your job is to **watch and write, not to help or teach.**

## 1. Welcome and consent (10 minutes)

Say (English; Odia and Hindi below):

> "Namaste. Thank you for giving us your time. We are making a phone app that helps a shopkeeper write down the day's cash and udhar. Today I would like you to try it on this phone. **We are testing the app, not you.** If something is difficult, that is the app's mistake, and that is exactly what helps us. There is no wrong answer. It will take about an hour. First I will read you a paper about what we write down, and you can say yes or no to each thing."

**Odia (DRAFT):**
> "ନମସ୍କାର। ଆମକୁ ସମୟ ଦେଲେ ଧନ୍ୟବାଦ। ଆମେ ଏକ ଫୋନ୍ ଆପ୍ ବନାଉଛୁ, ଯାହା ଦୋକାନୀଙ୍କୁ ଦିନର ନଗଦ ଓ ଉଧାର ଲେଖିବାରେ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରେ। ଆଜି ମୁଁ ଚାହେଁ ଆପଣ ଏହି ଫୋନ୍‌ରେ ଏହାକୁ ଚେଷ୍ଟା କରନ୍ତୁ। **ଆମେ ଆପ୍‌କୁ ପରୀକ୍ଷା କରୁଛୁ, ଆପଣଙ୍କୁ ନୁହେଁ।** କିଛି କଷ୍ଟ ଲାଗିଲେ ତାହା ଆପ୍‌ର ଭୁଲ୍, ଆଉ ସେହିଟା ହିଁ ଆମକୁ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରେ। କୌଣସି ଉତ୍ତର ଭୁଲ୍ ନୁହେଁ। ପ୍ରାୟ ଏକ ଘଣ୍ଟା ଲାଗିବ। ପ୍ରଥମେ ମୁଁ ଆମେ କଣ ଲେଖୁ ସେ ବିଷୟରେ ଏକ କାଗଜ ପଢ଼ି ଶୁଣାଇବି, ଆଉ ପ୍ରତ୍ୟେକ କଥା ପାଇଁ ଆପଣ ହଁ ବା ନା କହିପାରିବେ।"

**Hindi (DRAFT):**
> "नमस्ते। हमें समय देने के लिए धन्यवाद। हम एक फ़ोन ऐप बना रहे हैं जो दुकानदार को दिन का नकद और उधार लिखने में मदद करता है। आज मैं चाहता हूँ कि आप इसे इस फ़ोन पर आज़माएँ। **हम ऐप को परख रहे हैं, आपको नहीं।** कुछ मुश्किल लगे तो ग़लती ऐप की है, और यही हमारे काम आता है। कोई जवाब ग़लत नहीं है। करीब एक घंटा लगेगा। पहले मैं आपको एक कागज़ पढ़कर सुनाऊँगा कि हम क्या लिखते हैं, और हर बात पर आप हाँ या नहीं कह सकते हैं।"

Then:
1. Read `CONSENT.md` section A / B / C **aloud, in full**, in the participant's language.
2. Stop after section 3 (the table) and ask each of items 3 to 8 **one by one**: "Do you agree to [item]? Yes or no. Either is fine." Tick the sheet. Do not look disappointed at a "no".
3. Read sections 7 and 9 again in one sentence: "You can say stop at any moment, and you do not have to say why. You keep the thank-you."
4. Hand over the printed copy.
5. **Only now** switch on the recorders that have a "yes". Say what you switched on. Keep the screen recorder's indicator visible if the phone shows one.
6. Say: "Please do not use your real customers' names or amounts. Everything today is pretend. The shop on the phone is called Demo Kirana."

If any required box is "no", thank them, give the thank-you, and end.

## 2. Warm-up: how they close their day today (8 minutes)

Aim: how they really do it, in their words, for context and for T6. Do not suggest answers. Do not look at or write down real customers' names or amounts. Do not photograph any book.

Probes (use only these, to keep every session the same): "Tell me more." / "What happened next?" / "Why was that?" / "Can you show me with your hands how you do it?" (hands, not the book).

Write their own words for: cash, udhar, expense, closing, mismatch. Those are the words the redesign should use (and the native reviewer should check).

Settings to note (do not ask them to change): their phone's text size, whether they use glasses. Set the test phone's text size to match (see `SETUP.md`).

## 3. Think-aloud instructions and practice (3 minutes)

Say:

> "While you use the app, I would like you to say out loud what you see, what you are thinking, and what you are trying to do. For example, 'I am looking for the button to write a sale.' If you are stuck, say that too. I will mostly stay quiet and write. That is not because you are doing it wrong. **I cannot help you during the tasks**, even if you ask. I will help at the end. If you want to stop, tell me."

**Odia (DRAFT):**
> "ଆପ୍ ଚଲାଇବା ବେଳେ ଆପଣ ଯାହା ଦେଖୁଛନ୍ତି, ଯାହା ଭାବୁଛନ୍ତି, ଆଉ ଯାହା କରିବାକୁ ଚାହୁଁଛନ୍ତି, ତାହା ଜୋରରେ କହନ୍ତୁ। ଯେମିତି, 'ମୁଁ ବିକ୍ରି ଲେଖିବାର ବଟନ୍ ଖୋଜୁଛି।' ଅଟକି ଗଲେ ସେଇଟା ବି କହନ୍ତୁ। ମୁଁ ପ୍ରାୟତଃ ଚୁପ୍ ରହି ଲେଖିବି। ଆପଣ ଭୁଲ୍ କରୁଛନ୍ତି ସେଥିପାଇଁ ନୁହେଁ। **କାମ ବେଳେ ମୁଁ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରିପାରିବି ନାହିଁ**, ଆପଣ ପଚାରିଲେ ବି। ଶେଷରେ ମୁଁ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରିବି। ବନ୍ଦ କରିବାକୁ ଚାହିଁଲେ ମୋତେ କହନ୍ତୁ।"

**Hindi (DRAFT):**
> "ऐप चलाते समय आप जो देख रहे हैं, जो सोच रहे हैं और जो करना चाहते हैं, वह ज़ोर से बोलिए। जैसे, 'मैं बिक्री लिखने का बटन ढूँढ रहा हूँ।' अटक जाएँ तो वह भी बोलिए। मैं ज़्यादातर चुप रहकर लिखूँगा। इसलिए नहीं कि आप ग़लत कर रहे हैं। **काम के दौरान मैं मदद नहीं कर सकता**, आप पूछें तब भी। आखिर में मैं मदद करूँगा। रुकना चाहें तो मुझे बताइए।"

**Practice (not on the app):** hand them their own phone, or a neutral object, and say: "Please tell me out loud what you do to call someone from your phone." Or, if no phone: "Tell me out loud how you would find a packet of tea on your shelf." (Odia: "ଆପଣ ନିଜ ଫୋନ୍‌ରୁ କାହାକୁ କେମିତି ଫୋନ୍ କରନ୍ତି, ଜୋରରେ କହି କହି ଦେଖାନ୍ତୁ।" Hindi: "अपने फ़ोन से किसी को कैसे फ़ोन करते हैं, बोलते-बोलते दिखाइए।") Praise the talking, never the correctness.

Think-aloud may be unnatural for some people, especially older shopkeepers. If they go silent, use only: "What are you looking at?" / "What are you thinking?" Do not push. If they cannot think aloud, drop it and rely on observation and the post-task question.

Note on digits: the cards and the app show Latin digits (120, 350, 2,270). The Odia prompt above uses Odia digits for the spoken prompt only. Say the number aloud as you naturally would. Log if the participant reads a number wrongly, in particular 9 and Odia numerals.

### While they work: what you may and may not do

**You may** (not counted as an assist):
- Neutral encouragement: "Mm-hm." "Take your time." "Please keep talking."
- Neutral questions: "What are you thinking?" "What would you expect to happen?" "What are you looking for?"
- Repeat the task (once) if they forgot the number: point at the card.
- Tell them a time-box is up: "Let's try the next one together." (task = F)

**You must not:**
- Name a control, a screen or a word the app uses.
- Say "tap", "press", "scroll", "swipe", "it is at the bottom", "look there".
- Confirm that they are right or wrong ("good", "correct", a nod, a laugh at a mistake). Say "Thank you" after every task alike.
- Explain how the app works, or what a message means.
- Apologise for the app.
- Answer "is this right?". Say "What do you think?"
- Complete any step for them (except as Hint-3 below).

### Hint ladder (any hint = an assist; write the level and the time)

Hints are for when someone is truly stuck, because a person who sits frustrated teaches us nothing more. **Wait first**: let them try for at least 20 seconds of real stuck-ness (or half the time-box for T1, T3, T4), then:

| Level | What you may say | Counts as |
|---|---|---|
| Hint-0 | Neutral, no content: "What would you do next?" "What are you looking for?" | **Not** an assist. Still note it. |
| Hint-1 | A general nudge: "Look at the whole screen once more." "Is there anything on a different part of the screen?" | Assist (level 1) |
| Hint-2 | Point at an area, not the control: "Look at the lower part." "Look at the top." | Assist (level 2) |
| Hint-3 | Name the control, or do it: "That one." | Assist (level 3) and **F** if the task could not continue without it |

Record **count and highest level** for each task. After Hint-3 or at the time-box, say: "Thank you, let us do the next one together", and move on. If a task fails, use the checkpoint (`SETUP.md`) so that the next task starts correctly.

### Quick "how was it?" after every task (SEQ, 30 seconds)

Say: "Thank you. Now one question. How easy or how hard was that, from 1 to 7? 1 means very hard, 7 means very easy." Show the 1-to-7 card (see `OBSERVATION-SHEET.md`; use the picture version for non-readers, with seven steps). Write the number. Do not argue with it. Then one question only: "What made it that way?" (Odia: "ଏମିତି କାହିଁକି ଲାଗିଲା?" Hindi: "ऐसा क्यों लगा?") and write the answer in their words.

**Odia (DRAFT):** "ଧନ୍ୟବାଦ। ଏବେ ଗୋଟିଏ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ। ଏହା କେତେ ସହଜ ବା କଷ୍ଟକର ଥିଲା, ୧ ରୁ ୭ ଭିତରେ କହନ୍ତୁ। ୧ ମାନେ ବହୁତ କଷ୍ଟ, ୭ ମାନେ ବହୁତ ସହଜ।"
**Hindi (DRAFT):** "धन्यवाद। अब एक सवाल। यह कितना आसान या मुश्किल था, 1 से 7 में बताइए। 1 मतलब बहुत मुश्किल, 7 मतलब बहुत आसान।"

### Task order

T0 First launch, then T1, T2, T3, T4, T5, T6, T7 (see `TASKS.md`). Reset between tasks only where `TASKS.md` says so. Between T6 and T7 the moderator switches the app to English (checkpoint R4).

After T6, ask (not timed): "How would you close this on paper tonight? How long would it take?" (Odia: "ଆଜି ରାତିରେ ଏହାକୁ କାଗଜରେ କେମିତି ବନ୍ଦ କରନ୍ତେ? କେତେ ସମୟ ଲାଗନ୍ତା?" Hindi: "आज रात इसे कागज़ पर कैसे बंद करते? कितना समय लगता?")

## 7. If something goes wrong

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Participant gets upset, tired or wants to stop | Stop at once. "Thank you. You have helped a lot." Pay the thank-you. No questions. |
| A customer comes to the counter | Pause the timer and recorders. Let them serve. Resume with "Shall we continue?" Do not record the customer. |
| Phone freezes or app crashes | Note the time and what was on screen. Restart. If it is a build bug, mark `BUILD` in the sheet, restore the checkpoint, and drop that task's timing. |
| Participant types real data (own number, a real customer's name) | Say "that is fine, we will delete it". Reset the phone at the end. Do not copy it. |
| Participant asks for help with their own accounts | Do not give accounting advice. Thank them. After the session, say you cannot, honestly. |
| Participant asks "should I buy this?" or about price | "I do not know yet. I am not selling it today." No promises. |
| Dispute about payment or consent | Pay the thank-you; remove them from the data; refer to the grievance contact. |
| You made an error (said a hint out loud, hint unlogged) | Write it in the sheet at once with the time. Mark SA. Honest data matters more than a clean sheet. |
