# SCREENER: who qualifies for the usability sessions

Status: DRAFT for Saket. Nothing here has been sent to anyone. The researcher never contacts a participant; Saket (or someone he trusts) recruits and runs the screener.

Purpose: find 5 to 8 real shopkeepers in Odisha who look like the person in repo `PLAN.md` section 2 (middle-aged to elderly, Odia first, sometimes Hindi, numerate but not necessarily literate, budget phone, never used an accounting product). Recruit **10** so that 8 turn up.

Why a screener at all: every UI judgement so far is expert or agent review (CPO plan, "Facts we start from"). If we test with friends, relatives or tech-savvy shopkeepers, the sessions will say the app is fine because they are polite or fast. The screener is what protects the result.

---

## 1. Who qualifies (all must be true)

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Owns or runs a small retail shop and makes the daily money decisions | He is the user |
| Q2 | Shop has traded for at least 1 year | Has a real closing routine |
| Q3 | Gives goods on udhar (credit) to regular customers | The udhar tasks mean nothing otherwise |
| Q4 | Does or supervises a daily cash and udhar record: paper book, memory, or nothing. At most 1 participant who already uses another bookkeeping app | We test first-time use; one app user gives a comparison, more would bias |
| Q5 | First language Odia (6 to 7 of 8) or Hindi (1 to 2 of 8) | Coverage of the two draft copy sets |
| Q6 | 18 or older, and in practice 30 to 65+ | Consent must be the participant's own |
| Q7 | Can use a smartphone at least for calls. Low familiarity is welcome | The real target handset user |
| Q8 | Agrees to a session of about 60 to 75 minutes in or near the shop, on our test phone | Practical |
| Q9 | Has not seen, used or been shown SWAFTO or Retail Desk | First-time use is the measurement |

## 2. Who does NOT qualify (any one is enough)

- Has seen, used, been demoed or heard a walkthrough of SWAFTO / Retail Desk.
- Employee, contractor, intern or advisor of the company, or a close family member or close friend of staff. (Friends of Saket also: politeness bias. If Saket cannot avoid it, flag the participant `KNOWN-TO-FOUNDER` in the tracker and report results with and without them.)
- Works in software, app design, UX, market research or accounting software.
- Took part in a paid research session of any kind in the last 6 months (professional participant).
- Shop is a franchise or chain outlet that must use the franchisor's billing system.
- Does not extend any credit to customers.
- Cannot give consent for themselves.

Decline politely and pay nothing, since nothing was done. Say: "Thank you, this study needs a different kind of shop this time." Do not say which rule failed. Delete the screener answers of anyone who does not qualify within 7 days (see section 7).

## 4. Screener questions (read aloud, tick the answer, do not explain the product)

Say first: "I am [name]. I am asking a few questions to see if you can help us with a study about shop accounts. It takes 5 minutes. You can stop any time. I will not ask for your sales figures or customers' names."

| # | Ask (English; ask in Odia or Hindi) | Answers | Qualify rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Do you own this shop, or decide its daily money matters? | Yes / No | Yes |
| S2 | What do you sell? | write type | note for quota |
| S3 | How many years has the shop been running? | write years | 1 or more |
| S4 | Do you give goods on udhar to some regular customers? | Yes, many / Yes, a few / No | Not "No" |
| S5 | How do you keep your daily cash and udhar record today? | Paper book / Memory only / Another app (which? write) / Other | Not more than 1 "another app" overall |
| S6 | Who closes the day (counts the cash)? | Me / Me with family / Someone else only | Me, or me with family |
| S7 | Which language are you most comfortable speaking and reading? | Odia / Hindi / English / Other | Odia or Hindi (quota) |
| S8 | When you get a message in your own language, can you read it: easily / slowly / only numbers and my name? (Never ask anyone to read aloud.) | Easily / Slowly / Only numbers | Quota band: Easily = High, Slowly = Medium, Only numbers = Low |
| S9 | What do you do on your phone? Calls / WhatsApp messages / WhatsApp voice / UPI payments (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm) / YouTube / I install apps myself | multiple | Low = calls only or WhatsApp only; Medium = WhatsApp plus UPI; High = installs apps |
| S10 | Which age group are you in? 30 to 39 / 40 to 49 / 50 to 64 / 65 and over / under 30 | one | 30 or over; quota |
| S11 | Have you ever seen, used or been shown an app called SWAFTO or Retail Desk? | Yes / No | No |
| S12 | Do you, or anyone close in your family, work for or with the company [company name]? Do you work in apps, software, design or research? | Yes / No | No |
| S13 | Have you taken part in a paid study or interview in the last 6 months? | Yes / No | No |
| S14 | Do you wear glasses for close work, or find a phone screen hard to read in sunlight? (Not a disqualifier. Helps us bring the right set-up.) | Yes / No | quota note |
| S15 | Would you do a session of about an hour in or next to your shop, on a phone we bring, with a small thank-you from us? | Yes / No | Yes |
| S16 | (Optional, only if they wish) Tell us how you would like us to note your gender, or skip. | free / skip | quota note only |

Do not ask: exact income, turnover, number of customers by name, Aadhaar, bank or UPI details, caste, religion.

## 5. How to recruit (suggestions, Saket decides)

1. **Source:** local market associations, a wholesaler or distributor who knows many small shops, a bank or UPI business agent, a family-of-a-friend introduction **at one remove** (not the friend). Prefer people who do not know the company.
2. **Spread:** at least 2 towns or markets so that one local trader group does not dominate.
3. **Ask once, plainly.** No pressure to accept, no pressure to give a reason for declining.
4. **Mention the thank-you only as "a small thank-you amount"** until Saket fixes it (TBD). The same amount for everyone, paid whether or not they finish, and whether or not they agree to recording.
5. **Confirm** with a short message or call 1 day before: time, place, 60 to 75 minutes, bring glasses if they use them. Offer to move the time.
6. **Overbook:** 10 confirmed for 8 slots. Expect no-shows because shops cannot always leave the counter.
7. **Time of day:** off-peak for the shop (mid-morning or mid-afternoon). Sessions during the evening rush will be interrupted; that is realistic but not what this session measures.

## 6. After the screener

- Qualifies and agrees: fill the tracker row, book the session, send the confirmation. Keep the consent form (`CONSENT.md`) ready to read aloud at the start of the session. **Consent is NOT taken on the phone call**; it is taken at the session with the full itemised notice.
- Does not qualify: thank, end the call, record only "not eligible" with no reason, delete the answers within 7 days.
- Would like to withdraw before the session: no questions, delete their row.

## 7. The personal data in the screener (DPDP minimisation)

Minimum collected: first name or nickname, phone number (to schedule and to pay), the answers above. Rules:

- Name and phone number live on **one separate sheet** (paper or an encrypted file), never in the observation sheets, recordings, notes or synthesis.
- Delete the phone number within [PROPOSED: 30 days after the session or thank-you payment, whichever is later]. Saket confirms the number.
- Do not put the screener in WhatsApp groups, shared drives open to others, or cloud AI tools.
- Screener answers of non-qualifying people: delete within 7 days (**PROPOSED**).
- The legal text of the notice itself is in `CONSENT.md`; the screener call only tells the person what is asked and why, in one sentence.
