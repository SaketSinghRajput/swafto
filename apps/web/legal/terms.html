1 · What SWAFTO is

SWAFTO is a record-keeping tool: a day book for a small retail shop. It records what you type and adds it up.

It is not:

  • accounting software;
  • a tax product — it calculates no GST and produces no tax document;
  • a payment service — no money moves through it;
  • a lender, and it arranges no credit.

2 · Tax is yours

Nothing SWAFTO produces is prepared for a government submission, and none of it is warranted as fit for filing. Your tax compliance is yours: give your day book to your accountant, and do not treat it as a return.

3 · Your customers' details are your responsibility

When you record a customer's or supplier's name or phone number, you decide to collect it and we hold it on your instruction. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act you are the Data Fiduciary for that record, with the duties that follow — including telling those people what you keep, if they ask. We give you the tools: a customer's phone number is optional, you can export everything held about one customer, and you can remove their details.

4 · One shop, one phone

An account is one shop, and it is signed in on one phone at a time. Signing in on a new handset signs the old one out. This is a stated limit of the service and it is deliberate: a day book two devices can write to at once is a day book whose figures nobody can reconcile.

5 · What we promise, and what we do not

We stand behind the arithmetic. If SWAFTO adds up what you typed and gets it wrong, that is our defect and we will fix it. We do not disclaim that, because it is the whole point of the product and it is the part we test hardest.

What we cannot promise, and do not:

  • That what was typed in is complete or true. SWAFTO records what you enter. If an entry was never made, no total can know about it.
  • That the service is always available. The app works offline by design, so your book is on your phone; but sync, sign-in codes and this website depend on networks we do not own.
  • Anything about a business decision taken on the strength of a figure. Whether to extend credit, chase a debt or restock is your judgement, and its consequences are yours.

Outside the arithmetic, and to the extent the law allows, our liability is limited to what you have paid us for the service.

6 · Keeping your own copy

Your book lives on your phone and is backed up to our servers in India. Export it whenever you like. If you stop using SWAFTO, export it before you delete your account — after the 30-day grace period we cannot get it back for you.

7 · Ending it

You may delete your account at any time, in the app or at swafto.app/delete-account. We may suspend an account being used to attack the service or another user, and we will say why.

SWAFTO is offered to shop owners. It is not offered to anyone under the age of 18, and it is not designed for or directed at children.
