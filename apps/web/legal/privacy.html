SWAFTO privacy policy

Version 2026-09-1 · Last updated 21 September 2026

This text has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It was written to take the more protective reading wherever the law is uncertain, and it is published in that state deliberately rather than withheld. A reviewed version will replace it.

Who we are

SWAFTO is a day book for small retail shops. The Data Fiduciary for your account is SWAFTO. Our Grievance Officer can be reached at grievance@swafto.app and answers within 30 days.

What we collect, why, and for how long

This is the whole list. The same list is shown to you in the app before your account is created, and it is the list the software itself is built from — if something is not here, we do not collect it.

Purpose What we collect Why How long we keep it
Your account and your book
Required 		mobile number, shop name, the entries you type, customer and supplier names you enter to keep your day book, work offline, and restore it if you change phone kept while the account is in use; erased within 90 days of a deletion request, or of 24 months of inactivity
SMS reminders
Your choice 		mobile number to send your sign-in code and any reminders you ask for the number is kept only while the account exists
Crash reports
Your choice 		device model, app version, the point in the app where it stopped to fix crashes never includes an amount, a customer name or a phone number

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not put an amount, a customer name or a phone number into a crash report or a notification.
  • We do not guess. The app never invents a figure, completes an entry for you, or predicts what you meant to type.

Your customers' data

When you write a customer's name or number in your book, you are the one deciding to record it, exactly as you would in a paper ledger. In the language of the Act you are the Data Fiduciary for that record, and we process it on your instruction. We have built the tools that make that workable whichever way the analysis falls: a customer's phone number is always optional, you can export everything held about one customer, and you can remove a customer's details from your book.

Where your data is

In India. Our servers and our backups are in the AWS Mumbai region (ap-south-1). Your sign-in code is delivered by an Indian telecom operator under the TRAI DLT framework.

Your rights

  • See everything we hold about you. In the app, or by asking us.
  • Correct anything wrong. Your name, shop details and phone number are editable. Entries in a closed day are not, because a day book is a record of what was written at the time — you correct one the way every ledger has always been corrected, with a dated correction that refers back to it.
  • Have it erased. In the app, or at swafto.app/delete-account if you have already uninstalled it. Deletion takes effect after 30 days, and signing in before then cancels it. After that, encrypted backups rotate out within 90 days and are never restored.
  • Withdraw any optional consent, as easily as you gave it — the same switch, in the app, in one tap.
  • Complain. To our Grievance Officer first, and to the Data Protection Board of India if we do not resolve it.

If something goes wrong

If your data is exposed, altered or lost, we will tell you without delay — what happened, what it means for you, what we have done, what you can do, and who to contact — and we will report it to the Data Protection Board as the DPDP Rules require.

Children

SWAFTO is for shop owners. It is not offered to anyone under 18.

This is not a tax service

SWAFTO does not calculate GST, and nothing it produces is a tax document. What you export is your own day book, to give to your accountant.

Changes

If we change what we collect or why, we change this page and ask you again in the app. Every consent we hold records the version of the notice you agreed to, so we can always tell what you were told.
