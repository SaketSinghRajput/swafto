Delete your SWAFTO account

This deletes your book, not just the app. You do not need the app installed to do this.

What is deleted

  • Every day you closed, and every entry in it
  • Every customer and supplier you entered, and what they owe
  • Your shop name, your mobile number and your devices

What is kept, and for how long

  • A record that the deletion happened, with no name, number or amount in it. We keep it so we can show the deletion was carried out.
  • Encrypted backups rotate out within 90 days. They are never restored after a deletion.

You have 30 days to change your mind. Nothing is lost until then - sign in to the app again at any time before that day and the deletion is cancelled.

1 · Your mobile number

We send a six-digit code to the number the account is registered to. This code is only for deleting the account; it will not sign you in.

2 · The code, and the word DELETE

If something goes wrong

Write to grievance@swafto.app. We answer within 30 days. If we do not resolve it, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
