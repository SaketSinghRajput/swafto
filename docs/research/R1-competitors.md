# R1 · Competitors: apps for Indian small retailers, 2025-2026

*Desk research, 2026-10-26. Prices are quoted from the page linked next to each one,
and the text names the page's owner. Where a vendor page and a third-party page
disagree, both are given. Play Store ratings, review counts and install bands were
read from each app's Play listing on 2026-10-26 (`hl=en_IN&gl=IN`). Review themes
were counted by this author from Play's own review feed (method in §5). Anything
estimated says so and says who estimated it.*

**Scope ruling applied (product owner, 2026-10-26).** Neither plan, Free or Paid,
tracks per-product stock (stock levels, low-stock alerts, reorder). The comparison
table still records which competitors have it. It is **not** in the gap list and
**not** in the recommended split. Item-level capture per sale is a separate thing
(see R2's ruling) and is listed as a gap, flagged against D5.

## 0. Summary

1. **The free ledger is getting smaller.** In September and October 2026 the
   1-star Play reviews of **Khatabook** describe a new paid "Khatabook Plus" at
   ₹99 to ₹199 a month and a daily cap on free entries. Different reviewers give
   different caps: "3 entries per day", "6 entry din me". This report has not
   confirmed the cap or the price from Khatabook itself (§5). **OkCredit** charges
   ₹30 a month for "Unlimited Transactions" and ₹75 a month to remove ads
   ([okcredit.in/pricing](https://okcredit.in/pricing)). Its own comparison page
   still says the free plan has "unlimited entries"
   ([okcredit.in/khatabook-alternative](https://okcredit.in/khatabook-alternative)).
   The two pages contradict each other.
2. **The billing apps charge annually, ₹399 to ₹5,000 a year.** myBillBook Silver
   is ₹399 a year; Vyapar Mobile Silver/Gold is ₹699/₹799 a year; the Swipe PRO,
   JET and RISE plans are ₹2,799, ₹3,699 and ₹4,999 a year. Sources are in §2.
   They lock features behind number of devices and users, GST e-invoices and
   e-way bills, branding, and desktop access.
3. **Nobody sells a daily cash close.** No app reviewed has a cash check:
   expected cash in the drawer against cash counted, with a required reason for
   any difference. The nearest is Khatabook's separate "Cashbook" product
   ([khatabook.com notice](https://khatabook.com/discontinuing-the-mystore-app/)).
   In the sample, one 5-star Khatabook reviewer asks for the CashBook app back,
   and another asks for a Cash/Online/UPI payment-mode field on entries. SWAFTO
   already has both.
4. **What users punish:** a free tier that shrinks after they have built a
   history in it; ads inside the ledger; loan cross-selling and spam calls; sales
   calls after purchase; features removed or re-priced at renewal; buying again
   for a second device. The counts are in §5.
5. **The biggest gaps against SWAFTO** are not AI. They are: sending a customer an
   udhar reminder or statement on WhatsApp, app lock, data export, and a reminder
   to the shopkeeper about collections due. Every one can be done without
   inference, and all of them belong in **Free**.

**What the market treats as free:** a credit ledger, customers and suppliers,
basic reminders, unlimited invoices (Swipe), WhatsApp sharing, basic reports.
**What it charges for:** removing ads, a second device or a staff member, GST
compliance volume, desktop, branding, SMS volume, and now (Khatabook, OkCredit)
the number of entries.

Prices above are each vendor's or aggregator's figure. Whether GST is included
varies: Swipe says "inclusive of GST"; myBillBook says "Excl. GST @18%"; Vyapar
quotes "+ GST" on its partner price sheet.

### Deliberately not gaps

- **Voice billing, bill/parchi OCR, "AI fills the bill"** (Dukaan AI, Swipe AI,
  Vyapar TaxOne). Each puts a figure in the book that nobody typed, which the
  "No inference in data entry" rule forbids. It is SWAFTO's positioning against
  Dukaan AI, not a missing feature.
- **Photo attached to an entry** (Khatabook, Dukaan AI). The rule says "No camera
  … in the entry path". A photo kept as evidence is not inference, but it is still
  a camera. If wanted, the rule must be amended explicitly first. Not recommended
  for V1.
- **Loans inside the app** (Khatabook, PhonePe, Google Pay, BharatPe). This is the
  most common complaint in Khatabook's 1-star reviews (§5). Not recommended.
- **Inventory / stock**: excluded by the scope ruling.

## 4. Retention: what keeps users, and the honest version of each

What the 4-5★ reviews praise (§5): keeping **hisab** (records, tracking), **ease**,
and **time saved**. For Swipe, quick invoices. For Vyapar, one user praises
support that restored "4 saal ka bakup" (four years of backup). The 1-star reviews
are angriest at changes made *after* a user has years of data in the app: "5-6
saal se use kar raha hu … normal entry ki bhi limit lagadi hai" (Khatabook,
2026-10-13), "6 sal se use … har update ke bad extra money" (myBillBook,
2026-10-16). The user's own history is the retention, and it is also what gets
held hostage.

| Hook | Honest version for SWAFTO | Dark pattern to avoid (seen in market) |
|---|---|---|
| The daily ritual | The close itself, with T13's balanced moment. An opt-in local reminder at the shop's own closing time (decision 1), never more than once a day | Streak-loss guilt; notifications pushing upsells |
| Money recovered | GAP-1/GAP-3/GAP-6: reminders and statements that help collect udhar. Khatabook claims "3X faster" collection about itself ([Khatabook blog](https://khatabook.com/blog/small-business-accounting-apps/)), a vendor claim | Reminders that also spam the *customer* with offers |
| Trust in the history | Published pledge: **the free tier never shrinks; an entry once made is never paywalled; on lapse, paid features go read-only and nothing is deleted** (the Tap & Bill model above). Export always free (GAP-5) | Entry caps on existing users (Khatabook reviews); "removed many important features at renewal" (Vyapar review); price hikes at renewal (myBillBook review) |
| Observations that earn attention | Existing R1 behaviour indicators, stated as things to review ("credit outstanding has risen five business days running") | Fear-based "your business is at risk" prompts leading to a loan offer |
| Something for the lender or CA | A month-end summary the shopkeeper chooses to share (GAP-5 paid tier) | Reading SMS to target loans: "new update is demanding sms permision even when the app is not running" (Khatabook 1-star, 2026-09-24) |
| No ads | Free tier is ad-free | Ads inside the ledger (OkCredit's most common complaint) |
| Support after sale | Named grievance officer already required by DPDP (S6) | Sales calls on every invoice (Vyapar 1-star, 2026-09-23); support disappearing after payment (myBillBook, several) |

## 6. Recommended Free / Paid split

**Free, permanently, no ads, no entry or customer caps:**
- Everything SWAFTO does today: the nine sections, cash check and close, UPI
  confirmation, typed adjustments, returns/exchange, parties and udhar, Reports
  with indicators and scores, DPDP rights.
- GAP-1 WhatsApp reminders through the share intent · GAP-3 customer statement shared
  on WhatsApp · GAP-4 app lock · GAP-5 one-off export of your own data · GAP-6 collect-today
  notification · GAP-7 credit-limit observation.
- A published no-shrink pledge, and read-only (never deleted) on lapse.

**Paid** (each item either costs SWAFTO real money or needs infrastructure that
does not exist yet):
- GAP-2 server-sent SMS reminders · GAP-5 scheduled monthly pack for a CA or lender
- GAP-9 item-level sale capture (after D5 is superseded)
- GAP-10 second device / staff, GAP-13 desktop/web view, GAP-14 more shops (after D9 is
  revisited and the sync server exists)
- GAP-11 UPI receipt import, arriving unconfirmed

**Price anchor (an estimate by this author, not a measurement):** what the market
shows is ₹30-₹99 for 30 days (OkCredit) and ₹399-₹799 a year (myBillBook Silver,
Vyapar Mobile). A SWAFTO paid tier inside that band (roughly ₹49-₹99 a month, or
₹499-₹799 a year) would be priced like its peers. Khatabook reviewers say they
would accept "2 rupees per day upto 60" and "49 monthly". That is two reviewers,
not a willingness-to-pay study. R4 covers the law on subscriptions.

## 7. Risks and caveats

- **Khatabook's paywall comes from reviews, not from Khatabook.** The price (₹99
  or ₹199) and the cap (3 or 6 entries a day) differ between reviewers, and it may
  be an A/B test. Confirm by installing it before quoting it externally.
- **Prices conflict between sources.** myBillBook's Silver plan appears at both
  ₹399/yr and ₹199/mo; Swipe PRO at ₹2,799 (site) and ₹3,399 (App Store). Every
  figure is dated to the page cited.
- **Vendor self-claims** (Dukaan AI "#1", "100% offline", "24 languages";
  Khatabook "3X faster") are quoted as claims.
- **Odia support** is confirmed only for Swipe (and SWAFTO). For Khatabook,
  Vyapar, myBillBook and Dukaan AI it is "?" because no source names it.
  Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.
- **The opposing case:** the funded players bundle billing, GST and payments.
  A shop that needs GST invoices will need another app next to SWAFTO, and a
  second app is a churn risk. The gap list deliberately does not chase this, but
  it is the main reason a shopkeeper might leave.
- Earlier figures in `docs/spec/source/Strategy-Report.txt` (Khatabook 10M+
  monthly actives, funding totals) were not re-verified here.
