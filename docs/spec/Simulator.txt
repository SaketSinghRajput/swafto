1. Purpose
This simulator is an internal engineering tool, not a customer-facing product.
Its mission is to become the mathematical foundation of SWAFTO by allowing us to experiment
with business logic, validate formulas, and test real-world MSME scenarios before integrating
them into the mobile application.

2. Engineering Principles
     Stability before Scalability.
     Simplicity before Complexity.
     Transactions are Immutable.
     Business Closing is the primary calculation unit.
     Every output must answer a real business question.
     Every output must be reproducible from transaction data.
     The simulator must remain modular and easy to extend.

3. Recommended Architecture
Business Intelligence Engine
Containing:

     Formula Engine
     Cash Calculator
     Metrics Engine
     Business Health Engine
     Score Engine
     Recommendation Engine
     Scenario Simulator
This keeps all business calculations centralized and prevents logic from being scattered across
multiple modules.
