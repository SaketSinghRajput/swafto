SWAFTO · DECISIONS BEFORE PIXELS · 8 SEPTEMBER 2026

Nine calls to make before a
single screen gets generated.

The design prompt I wrote silently assumed answers to four of these. This
settles them properly, defines the one thing no screen list can express --
what a day actually is as it moves from open to closed -- and marks the
four questions that judgment cannot settle, with the metric that will settle
each once the app is live.

      Working conditions for these rulings

     No shopkeeper access right now, so every call below is made on the
     paper sheet, the arithmetic, and the platform. Where judgment could
     plausibly be wrong, the ruling takes the reversible option and the last
     section names the number that will prove it either way. Two of the
     nine aren't really decisions at all -- they're accounting facts that a
     well-meaning developer will otherwise "fix" back.

PART ONE -- THE REGISTER

The nine

Each one names the question, the call, and what changes on screen because of
it. Confidence is how sure I am. Reversible says what it costs to change your
mind in six months.

D1                              UPI stays manual in V1

  HIGH CONFIDENCE               Integrate UPI automatically, or type the totals?
  REVERSIBLE --
  CHEAP

                                Type them. UPI sales, opening UPI and closing UPI are
                                ordinary typed fields. Reconcile cash only. Store every UPI
                                figure with a source field -- manual or imported -- from
                                the first commit.

                                I assumed Play policy blocked this and I was wrong: Google explicitly
                                permits READ_SMS for "SMS-based financial transactions -- for
                                example, Unified Payments Interface (UPI)" and for money-
                                management apps that track budgets. So the door is open, and the
                                decision is a product one instead. Three reasons to still wait: the
                                permission needs a declaration and review round you don't want on
                                the critical path to launch; transaction SMS formats vary by bank and
                                PSP and break silently, which is exactly the failure mode a
                                correctness-first product cannot afford; and the shopkeeper's
                                PhonePe app already reconciles his UPI for him. Cash is the thing no
                                other app on his phone is counting. That source field is the entire
                                cost of keeping the door open.

                                ON SCREEN  UPI sales is a plain typed section. No connecting state, no
                                           permission prompt in onboarding, no "syncing..." spinner
                                           anywhere in V1.

D2                              Only Cash mode touches the cash equation

  NOT A PREFERENCE              What do the five expense modes do to the expected balance?
  ARITHMETIC
                                   Expense modes split in two. Cash affects the cash balance.
                                   UPI, Other, Udhar and Shop stock do not. All five count
                                   toward total expenses and toward profit.

                                An expense settled from shop stock is a real cost and not a cash
                                outflow. If it lands in cash-paid, every expected balance is wrong by
                                that amount and the shopkeeper's very first reconciliation fails for a
                                reason he cannot see -- which is the worst possible first experience
                                for a product whose whole promise is that the number is right.

                                ON SCREEN  The mode selector cannot be a flat row of five equal chips.
                                           Cash sits apart, or the other four carry a "doesn't change your
                                           cash" line. This is the screen most likely to be generated
                                           wrong.

D3                              Personal withdrawal is not an expense

  NOT A PREFERENCE              Where does the owner taking 500 for himself land?
  ACCOUNTING
                                   It reduces cash. It is never an expense and it never
                                   touches profit. Your sheet's formula -- net sales -
                                   purchase - expense -- is correct precisely because
                                   withdrawal is absent from it.

                                This is the single most common error in small-shop bookkeeping, and
                                it is the kind of thing a developer will confidently "correct" during a
                                refactor because it looks like money going out. It needs a Formula
                                Engine test that fails loudly and names the rule in its message.

                                ON SCREEN  Withdrawal never appears inside an expense list. On the day-
                                           closed summary it sits below the profit line, visually
                                           separated, not inside the subtraction.

D4                              Due dates are optional chips, not a terms field

  MEDIUM -- WATCH IT            How does the app know when a supplier payment is actually due?
  REVERSIBLE
                                   An optional due date on udhar-mode purchases, entered as
                                   quick chips -- 7 days · 15 days · 30 days · pick a date --
                                   remembered per supplier after first use. Never mandatory,
                                   never blocking.

                                Without something here, "Payment Due" can only say "23 days since
                                purchase", which is a fact rather than an alert. But kirana supply
                                terms are frequently informal -- "next week", "when the truck
                                comes" -- and a mandatory terms field would stall entry on a
                                question the shopkeeper often can't answer precisely. Chips let him
                                answer when he knows and skip when he doesn't. This is the ruling I
                                am least sure about, and the last section says how to find out.

                                ON SCREEN  One optional row on the purchase form. The aging badge
                                           reads "overdue" only where a date exists, and "31+ days"
                                           everywhere else -- two different words, deliberately.

D5                              No item or stock data in V1,
                                and no placeholder for it
  HIGH CONFIDENCE
  REVERSIBLE --                 Do we capture items so Inventory Pressure can exist?
  COSTLY
                                   No. Drop Inventory Pressure from the Reports list entirely --
                                   not greyed out, not "coming soon". "Top 3 items sold" stays
                                   the free-text diary line it already is, or goes.

                                Your own 15-07 note marks it "(future)", correctly. Item capture is the
                                one addition that meaningfully lengthens the day close, and day-
                                close duration is the number that will predict whether anyone still
                                uses this in week three. A greyed-out card is worse than no card: it
                                advertises an absence on a screen whose job is to make the
                                shopkeeper feel on top of things.

                                ON SCREEN Reports carries five metrics, not six. No locked inventory card.

D6                              A missed day is marked missed, never guessed

  HIGH CONFIDENCE               The shop was shut Thursday, or he just forgot. Then what?
  INTEGRITY
                                   Never interpolate a balance. On reopening after a gap, ask
                                   for one fresh cash count and mark the intervening days
                                    not closed . A not-closed day can still be closed late, but a
                                   late close never rewrites a later day's opening cash.

                                A ledger that quietly invents a number to bridge a gap is worse than
                                no ledger, because the shopkeeper has no way to know which figures
                                he can trust. Marking the gap costs one banner and buys the entire
                                credibility of every number after it.

                                ON SCREEN A third day-state on the day list, and a gap banner on home.
                                                  Neither existed in the screen list I sent you.

D7                              Open days are editable.
                                Closed days are immutable.
  HIGH CONFIDENCE
  SHAPES EVERYTHING             It's 9pm, the day is closed, and he realises this morning's cash sale was
                                680 not 860. Now what?

                                Inside an open day, edit anything, any line, freely. Once a
                                day is closed it is frozen. A correction is posted as a dated
                                adjustment on the current open day, carrying a reference
                                to the day it corrects.

                                Every closed day's counted cash is the next day's opening. Let
                                someone edit a closed day and you either silently invalidate every
                                balance after it or you cascade a rewrite through the chain -- and the
                                chain is the product. Real paper ledgers already work this way: you
                                don't erase an entry, you post a correction underneath it. It costs the
                                shopkeeper nothing he isn't used to, and it gives you an audit trail
                                for free. This is the biggest of the nine and it is the one my screen list
                                had no answer for at all.

                                ON SCREEN  Every closed-day view is read-only with a single "Post a
                                           correction" action. That's a whole screen the earlier prompt
                                           was missing.

D8                              Any order -- but "skipped" and
                                "empty" are different words
  HIGH CONFIDENCE
  REVERSIBLE                    Must the nine sections be filled in sequence?

                                Free order. Enter anything, any time, as the day happens.
                                Each section can be explicitly marked "Nothing to enter".
                                Cash Check unlocks only once all eight preceding sections
                                are entered or deliberately skipped.

                                A shopkeeper's expenses don't arrive in a tidy block at 9pm; a forced
                                sequence would mean the app only works if he sits down at the end
                                of the day, which is exactly the behaviour the paper sheet already
                                fails to produce. The important half of this ruling is the second half: a
                                day with genuinely zero expenses and a day where expenses
                                were forgotten produce the identical reconciliation number
                                and mean completely different things. If the app can't tell them
                                apart, neither can the shopkeeper, and neither can you when you're
                                debugging why his cash never matches.

                                ON SCREEN  Every section row has three states, not two: entered with a
                                           total, explicitly skipped, or not touched. The day list I
                                           specified had two.

D9                              Offline-first, and one shop means one phone

  HIGH CONFIDENCE               What happens with no signal, and what happens on a second device?
  SCOPE GUARD
                                   A local write always succeeds; sync happens in the
                                   background and never blocks an entry. V1 assumes one
                                   shop, one phone -- supporting two devices on one shop is
                                   an explicit non-goal.

                                Connectivity at a counter is unreliable and a cash entry that fails
                                because of signal is a lost transaction, permanently. Naming single-
                                device as a non-goal matters just as much: without it, someone will
                                spend three weeks building conflict resolution for a scenario that
                                does not occur in V1, and your resource plan does not have three
                                weeks.

                                ON SCREEN No blocking spinners on any entry path. A quiet sync indicator
                                                  at most. No merge-conflict UI anywhere.
