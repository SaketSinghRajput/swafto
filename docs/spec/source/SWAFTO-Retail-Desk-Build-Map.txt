01 -- THE COMPRESSION

Eight entry sections, four forms

The paper sheet has nine numbered sections, which reads like nine screens to
build. It isn't. Eight of them are entry, and those eight collapse into four form
shapes -- three of which carry almost everything. The ninth is the reconciliation
and is nothing like the others, which is why it gets its own treatment.

Section 9 is not a form

Cash check is a reading screen with one input. It shows the expected
balance as its working, takes a counted figure, and then either
congratulates or demands a reason. Treating it as a fifth form is the
mistake that turns the most important screen in the product into a data-
entry chore.

04 -- ORDER

What to build, in what order

Not by tab and not by screen count -- by what has to be true before the next thing
can be judged.

01 Send the twelve deltas to the design-system session first

           Seven new components and five changed shapes. That session is currently building
           against a brief that no longer matches, and every component it finishes before this
           reaches it is a component built to the wrong spec.

02 Finish Phase 1 of the design system

           Foundations, Money, Actions, Entry, Day close -- now with the corrected shapes.
           Nothing here depends on screens, and screens generated before it have no
           components to reference.

03 Design the four form archetypes, not the eight sections

           Forms A, B, C, D. Get the mode selector and the party field right once and eight
           screens are right by construction.

04 Design the cash check trio together

           Counting, difference-with-reason, and balanced. These three are the product; if
           they don't feel right nothing downstream saves it. Design them as one flow, never
           as three separate artboards.

05 Then the Today screen, last of the P0 set

           Counter-intuitive, but Today is a summary of everything above it. Design it once
           you know what it's summarising, or you'll design a dashboard and retrofit the
           product to it.

SWAFTO · Retail Desk Build Map                                                                    9
06 P1 only after one full day can be closed end to end

           Gap handling, corrections, day history, the withdrawal form. All of these are edges
           around a loop that has to work first.

      The one number to watch while building

     Fifteen P0 screens is the whole of a working day close. If that count
     starts climbing during design, something has been added that the nine
     decisions didn't call for -- and on current evidence the thing most likely
     to creep back in is a shortcut that saves the shopkeeper typing. That is
     exactly what was removed on purpose.
